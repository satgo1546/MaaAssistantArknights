石山糊出来了！连接配置touch type = "MaaFwAdb"，config = "AVD"即可使用，例如Python：

```python
asst = Asst()
asst.set_instance_option(InstanceOptionType.touch_type, "MaaFwAdb")
asst.connect("/usr/bin/adb", "emulator-5554", "AVD")
```
